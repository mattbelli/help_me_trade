read

Wall 5.5 is the balance point. Below it the outline reads as a different, flimsier object than its twin; above it the hole shrinks to a seam and the mark drifts back toward two solid bars — losing the entire reason the merge works. 5.5 on a 20-wide body is a 27% wall: worth writing down, because it's the ratio every future cut (app icon, watch, favicon) should preserve or consciously break.

Equalizing the footprints also quietly fixed the balance: the mark's optical center now sits between the bars instead of leaning left, so it will sit better next to the wordmark and centered in an app-icon tile.
