Organization (reusing what watchlist + chart already do): the top bar is the grid's WidgetHeader — title, a channel dot (hover the ⚙), and settings — so the widget body stays pure data (no second header). Scope lives in the ⚙: channel → follow the focused symbol like the chart (multiple flow widgets can lock to one channel), or watchlist / all (firehose) / a ticker list when disconnected. Columns are sortable (score default, click a header — the watchlist pattern); premium floor + type filter also live in the ⚙, out of your face.

The rows: left fill = magnitude, side-tinted (green buy / red sell) · score hover-inspectable · click a row → a plain dropdown of full depth (quote, Greeks, prints, OI, spark) — no inner card, the whole area is the drawer · flag = how we know the side: — live quote at ask/bid · polled 30s ≤30s quote, may shift · no side mid-spread, never guessed.
