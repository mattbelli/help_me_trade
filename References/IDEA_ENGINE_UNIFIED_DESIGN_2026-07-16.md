## Core mechanism (one paragraph, the essential shape)

The database is the event bus and the ledger is the gatekeeper. Existing detectors (flow, tape, RVOL, filings, levels, pressure gauge, strategy predicates) keep writing facts to their own stores across the real three-process topology (`hmt-backend`, `hmt-flow-collector`, `hmt-equity-collector`); a **trigger-intake job** cursor-polls those stores on integer-id cursors every 60s, scores each event with a per-class deterministic scorer, coalesces everything about one symbol into an **episode**, and stamps every qualifying event with exactly one counted **disposition** — a conservation law an auditor proves daily. Admitted episodes are investigated *immediately* (never held for a coalescing window): a deterministic **dossier** (claim matrix over perception facts + flow/tape/filings/social/portfolio/idea-history) is built, a cheap **T1 triage** call emits a null hypothesis, and **REASON v1** (one structured call) — with **REASON v2** (bull/bear debate) on a hash-sampled unbiased A/B, on escalation, or on top-importance episodes — produces a full plan under a server-enforced contract that has **no probability field to fill**. Every plan is registered in `idea_outcomes` at birth, before any delivery decision, alongside Path A strategy proposals (closing today's gap where rejected proposals vanish from science); a grader resolves every idea mechanically at the resolution its horizon demands, under a pre-registered grading law (pessimistic tie-breaks, `no_fill` as a separate tracked rate, cluster-tagged samples). Delivery is a *consequence of the ledger*: new sources ride labeled-"unproven" in inbox digests from birth, and only founder-armed sources whose cluster-adjusted Wilson cells cleared fixed-checkpoint gates earn Approve/Reject pushes through the existing `propose_trade` chokepoint seam — after a delivery-time staleness recheck. Liveness is proven, not inferred: dispositions must balance, a daily canary traverses detector→T2→ledger→**a real push with a delivery receipt**, the daily wrap is a guaranteed user-side heartbeat computed only from ledger queries, and an **external dead-man cron outside `hmt-backend`** escalates on a second channel when the wrap's delivery receipt is absent.

Detectors — existing, unchanged.** `signals/options_flow/*` (in `hmt-flow-collector`), `db/equity_tape` (in `hmt-equity-collector`), RVOL alerts, session-node scan, `signals/filings/edgar.py`/`congress.py`, `db/index_pressure`, `signals/strategy_scan.py`. Inputs: market data. Outputs: rows in their native stores (the spec's law: an event is a fact). Zero detector changes except Phase-1 registration hooks.

**2. Trigger intake + triage — NEW `signals/idea_triggers.py` + `db/idea_triggers.py`.** Cursor-polls the WAL SQLite stores on **integer-id cursors** (never `created_at` arithmetic — `options_flow_events.created_at` is an ISO string; the epoch-ms trap is documented). This is the only transport that survives the process split: an in-process hook in `hmt-backend` would never see collector-side events, and `notify()` is push-budget-lossy. Responsibility: per-class deterministic scoring, episode coalescing, dedup/cooldown, the priority queue, storm shedding, dispositions. Registered as a `Job` in `api/jobs.py` (`market_hours_only=True`). Rows are written **only for events clearing the per-class meaningful gate**; below-gate volume becomes per-class daily counters in `lane_stats` (never a second copy of the firehose). Queue and cursors are tables — restarts lose nothing; a startup reconciler sweeps rows stranded `investigating` by a mid-day deploy into `orphaned_restart` (counted, alarmed only if recurrent).

**3. Dossier builder — NEW `signals/dossier.py` (spec §4).** Pure code, no LLM. Wraps `ai/perception` `get_context(scope="symbol")` and adds flow events, tape prints, filings, social buzz (`db/social_mentions`), portfolio interest, and **the symbol's own graded idea history** as a first-class angle. Output: persisted claim matrix (`idea_dossiers`) with freshness + contradiction links + token-budgeted summary. Built only for admitted episodes — never per raw event, so the open storm costs zero external fetches.

**4. Investigator — NEW `research/reason.py` + `idea_investigator` Job.** T1 triage (null hypothesis), REASON v1, REASON v2 (`ai/research/engine_v2.run_research_v2` with a read-only capped tool belt), T3 adversary. Every call resolves to exactly one of `{ok, parse_fail, refusal, timeout, budget_denied}` persisted **before the response is used**, plus an `llm_spend` row. No trading toolset exists in the roster, ever (constraint 1: outputs terminate at `register_idea` and the delivery gate; the only broker path remains the deterministic chokepoint behind human approval).

**5. Contract validator — EXTEND `ai/research/ideas.validate_ideas`.** Rejects malformed plans, wrong-side stops (short-side inversion lands here; today's code is long-only at `ai/research/ideas.py:93` — Phase 1 stays long-only), any numeric probability (`_has_fabricated_probability`), empty bear case, unknown keys, and cited-claim integrity: claim **ids strict**, claim **values tolerant** (strict value-matching would manufacture parse failures and poison the alarm's credibility).

**6. Ledger + grader — EXTEND `db/idea_outcomes.py` + `research/idea_tracker.py`.** New singular `register_idea` wrapper (the existing surface is plural `register_ideas` at line 178; the spec's naming is reconciled by adding the wrapper); spec §2 columns plus `episode_id`, `grading_resolution`, `fill_status`, `cluster_key`, `synthetic`, `user_id` (single row today). **Path A registration**: `propose_trade`/`auto_execute_entry` call `register_idea(origin=f"strategy:{rule_id}")` fail-soft (registration failure never blocks a proposal — the ledger is downstream of trading, never a gate on it); approve/reject/expire in `trading/proposals.py` stamps `user_action = taken|skipped`. Grading per the Grading Law below.

**7. Calibration — NEW `signals/idea_calibration.py`** (spec §6 math verbatim: canonical Wilson, z=1.96, one implementation) extended with cluster-effective n, hierarchical cell fallback, and fixed-checkpoint evaluation (below). Read-time pure functions only.

**8. Delivery gate + composer — NEW `signals/idea_delivery.py`.** `compose_idea_payload(idea_id)` is the **only constructor of user-facing idea payloads** — phone card, digest entry, widget row, and chat tool output all consume this one serializer. Its schema **requires** `calibration:{status, wilson_lb?, n, n_eff, fill_rate}`; `send` refuses (and counts `send_refused`) any payload missing it — the 2026-07-08 transport-stripping leak class becomes a hard send-time failure. Also: delivery-time staleness recheck, push-vs-digest-vs-silent routing, `_peek_push_slot` budgets, episode-level dedup (A/B siblings render as one card + a "second opinion differs: [delta]" line; higher tier wins).

**9. Auditor / liveness plane — NEW `signals/pipeline_audit.py`, run as its own lightweight unit/cron (the `hmt-flow-collector` split is the precedent), plus an external dead-man cron.** Conservation checks (all counts are **queries over the ledger/trigger tables, never parallel counters** — a self-maintained counter that always printed 0 is the founding incident), rate-based anomaly rules vs trailing calendar-aware baselines, canary injection, cost meter, config-hash reporting.

## Event taxonomy + triage (what counts as meaningful, importance scoring, dedup/cooldown, storm behavior)

**Taxonomy — per-class deterministic scorers, one each, unit-tested** (never one overloaded `score_confluence`; a filing has no flow components to score):

| Class | Meaningful when | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| `flow.convergence` | always (≥2 aligned signals) | direct-queue T2-eligible |
| `flow.print` | premium ≥ p95 vs **this symbol's** trailing distribution | T1 |
| `tape.block`/`tape.velocity` | notional/velocity ≥ p95 per-symbol | T1 |
| `rvol.spike` | ≥3× time-of-day expected with same-day move | T1 |
| `filing.8k`/`congress.trade` | Item 2.02/8.01; large trades; scorer = item type + interest + RTH proximity | T1 |
| `index.pressure` | \|z\| ≥ 2 vs session history | record-only until the 07-17 calibration verdict, then T1 |
| `strategy.predicate` | fires | register-only (already fully planned mechanically; no LLM re-run) |
| `level.touch`, `earnings.proximity` | — | modifiers only, never standalone triggers |

Per-symbol percentile normalization is multi-user-safe (no hardcoded tickers; watchlist is a runtime boost, never a gate). Cold-start symbols with no trailing distribution fall back to a global per-class prior; the distribution-refresh job carries its own heartbeat — it is a lane like any other.

**Importance (0–100, deterministic, all terms journaled):** magnitude percentile (0–60) + **confluence bonus** (+10 per additional *independent angle family* on the episode) + interest boost (held-by-any-user +10, watchlist +5 — computed from the union of user interest sets, a delivery-blind global) + catalyst proximity (+10) − re-description penalty (−20 if a graded idea on this symbol resolved <5 sessions ago). **Independence is defined, not assumed**: flow prints, tape blocks, and RVOL arising within the same 5-minute window on one symbol are one *participation* family (one whale order is all three observed three ways — the pseudo-confluence trap); families = participation / levels / catalyst / filings / social / index-context, and inter-family correlation is measured from the ledger so the composite class earns its multiplier as a graded class rather than receiving it by taxonomy. On macro days a **cross-sectional discount** applies: importance is scored relative to the concurrent episode population, so "everything is anomalous" reads as "nothing is" and the escalation budget isn't drained by 09:40 on the least-informative morning of the month.

**Episodes + dedup ("new information, not louder same information"):** first qualifying event opens an episode; investigation fires **immediately** (resolving the window-vs-latency contradiction: the coalescing window governs *attachment*, never investigation timing). Later events attach and raise importance; if a **new angle family** attaches post-investigation or importance jumps a full band, exactly one re-investigation is allowed (≥60 min apart, max 2 investigations + 3 total per symbol/day). Owned cost: the first investigation may miss later confluence; the escalation path covers it, and n-honesty is double-walled — `episode_id` on every idea plus the spec §1 `dedup_key` (`origin:symbol:direction:bucket`, partial-unique over open rows).

**Storm behavior (the open, Fed days):** intake never calls an LLM inline and never fetches externally — a 500-event burst costs milliseconds of store reads. Time-of-day-conditioned baselines gate storm mode (every open exceeds an unconditioned p99); under storm: coalescing windows widen, bars rise, a **60–90s micro-batch replaces FIFO** so T2 slots go to top-k by score rather than arrival order, a **deterministic beta-residual screen** (computable from bars, no LLM at 9:31) separates index-beta movers from idiosyncratic ones before any cohort call, and a **reserved opening sub-budget** (25% of daily) stops the bell from draining the day. All shedding is explicit and counted (`shed_storm`, `dropped_budget`); a 90-min trigger TTL (`expired_unprocessed`) ensures a stale event never spawns a fresh-looking idea. User price alerts and Path A cards bypass the queue entirely — the queue governs only LLM investigations, so no user-alert path is ever slowed.

## LLM invocation design (tiers, budgets, tools given to it, prompt inputs, output contract, parse-failure behavior)

**Tier 0 — deterministic (free).** Scoring, episodes, dossiers. Under LLM outage or exhausted budget, the deterministic structure detector (`ai/research/setup_scan` pullback/breakout, R/R ≥ 1.5) keeps minting `origin="structure_event"` ideas — the calibration flywheel never stops at zero LLM cost.

**Tier 1 — batch triage (cheap role via the provider factory).** One structured call per batch of ≤8 candidates; inputs are event facts + store reads only. Output per candidate: `{investigate: bool, null_hypothesis: str, angle_to_check: str}` — the null hypothesis ("likely a hedge/roll — check OI and the expiry ladder") is what forces T2 into genuine checking rather than template-filling. **100% of T1 dismissals are shadow-registered** as class-template ideas (they already cleared the triage bar — the dismissal frontier is the highest-selection-bias boundary in the system); the drop floor below triage gets an ε≈2% shadow sample. Budget ≤150/day.

**Tier 2 — investigation.** Dossier first, then:
- **REASON v1** (`origin="reason_v1"`): one structured call, cheap model. Inputs: dossier summary + claim matrix, T1's null hypothesis, regime tag, holdings/exposure, next earnings date, and the cell's graded history stated as mechanical fact (feeding calibration *to* the LLM is fine; honesty constrains flow *from* it). Budget ≤40/day.
- **REASON v2** (`origin="reason_v2"`): engine_v2 bull/bear specialists + critic, read-only tools hard-capped at 8 tool calls + 120s wall clock + per-call token caps (`finish_reason=length` is a contract failure, never silence — the DeepSeek lesson): `get_context`, flow/tape/filings/social queries, SearXNG, earnings calendar, positions, `get_idea_history`. Fires on three tagged populations — `sample_reason ∈ {hash_ab, importance, escalation}`: a deterministic hash of `episode_id` selects ~20% of all investigated episodes for the unbiased A/B; importance ≥70; T1/v1 escalation (its own origin `reason_v2_escalated`). **The v1-vs-v2 comparison filters `hash_ab` only**, with per-sub-lane budget reservations so exhaustion (which correlates with time of day) can't shed the A/B sample non-randomly, and a pre-registered arbitration rule (which cells, what n, review date) so both origins don't run forever at double cost. Budget ≤12/day, concurrency 1.
- **T3 adversary**: for ideas within ±10 points of the push bar or above-median size *with multi-day horizons* (fast-decay ideas get an inline adversarial section inside T2 instead — latency lands where it's affordable), **plus a fixed-rate random audit across all ideas** (a friction-triggered-only audit is Goodhart-bait: the model learns to include one token neutral row). T3 can veto or harden the bear case; never upgrade.

**Output contract (server-enforced):**
```
{ verdict: "idea" | "no_idea",
  direction, entry_zone: {low, high}, stop, targets[1..3], plan_horizon,
  confidence: "low"|"medium"|"high",          # categorical ONLY, stored as the string
  thesis, bear_case,                          # empty bear_case => REJECT
  angles: [ {claim_id, stance: supports|contradicts|neutral} ],  # >=3; ids strict, values from dossier
  null_hypothesis_result: str }               # must address T1's null
```
No probability/win%/score field exists; the validator rejects unknown keys — LLM probabilities are unrepresentable, not policed. Angle **values render from the dossier claims deterministically**; the LLM contributes stance and interpretation only, so it cannot misstate a number under a real tool-call id. `no_idea` annotates the trigger row and never pollutes `idea_outcomes` (an idea is a plan; an event is a fact). The categorical confidence maps to `{low:0.35, medium:0.55, high:0.70}` **at read time inside the Brier function only** — the numeric never persists, so the perception layer and chat can't launder "0.70" into "70% confidence" (the exact transport pattern of the 07-08 leak).

**Parse-failure behavior (the incident class, triple-walled):** validate → one repair retry with the specific error echoed → disposition `parse_failed`, **no idea registered, no fallback fabricated** (the deterministic `_fallback_plan` remains only as `structure_event`'s own origin, never a disguise for a failed call) — but the failed investigation **is graded against the class template** so the model failing preferentially on hard setups can't bias surviving samples upward. Alarms are **rates against trailing baselines, not booleans-at-zero** (a 90%-dead lane that squeezes out one idea a day must page): parse-fail rate >20%/session or 3 consecutive → founder push; investigated/queued ratio collapsing vs the 10-day calendar-aware median → founder push; the day-end rules read the daily `lane_stats` counters, not current row status (the TTL must not destroy the evidence before the rule runs).

## Ping/summary UX (what the phone shows, when it pings vs batches vs stays silent, worked example)

**Four delivery lanes, strictly separated:**
1. **Armed push (Approve/Reject)** — only when ALL hold: source founder-armed for push; importance ≥ bar; delivery-time staleness recheck passes; a push slot is available (5 idea-pushes/day, priority displacement). Routes through `propose_from_scan_fire → propose_trade → create_phone_proposal → send_proposal_push` + Live Activity — inheriting kill-switch, market-hours, dedup, and `RiskEngine.validate` pre-check because it *is* that seam. Never delayed on the assumption the human is slow.
2. **Digest (inbox-only notification, 3×/day: ~10:00, 13:00, 16:15 ET off `market_calendar`)** — every registered idea not push-worthy, **including unproven-source ideas explicitly labeled "unproven — apprentice (n=k, graded not guessed)"**. Resolution with reason: the arming ladder gates *push*, not digest — over-gating digests starves trust for the first two months while cells accrue, and house doctrine says honesty constrains presentation, not construction. Digest assembly re-runs the freshness check; stale plans demote with `expired_undelivered` (graded — that is the measured cost of delivery latency).
3. **Silent ledger** — below-bar ideas, widget-only.
4. **Protective lane** — halt/gap-through-stop/filing on a *held* name: exempt from all batching, cooldowns, and budgets; per-position hysteresis latch (one ping per level-crossing, re-armed on meaningful recovery; multiple at-risk positions batch into one push); **unmutable** (mutes scope to opportunity classes only).

**The card** (full card fetched by the app — the push carries symbol + one line + the compact calibration token, because iOS truncates and paraphrases; the qualifier is structurally inseparable from the number: `win floor 58% · n=23`, never a bare percent): WHY NOW (detector evidence refs — no LLM authorship of magnitudes) / ANGLES (deterministic values + LLM stance, contradictions rendered at equal visual weight) / PLAN (entry zone, stop, targets, horizon, machine-checkable invalidation — which may retract pings, **never trade**) / BEAR / SIZE (deterministic sleeve-fraction rule, RiskEngine preview stamped "sized at HH:MM", indicative) / HONESTY (Wilson lower bound + n + fill rate when the cell cleared its gate, else the literal word **"unproven (accruing n=k)"** — **no W/L split below the gate**; the user must not be able to compute a sub-gate percentage in his head) / dual timestamps (price-at-trigger AND price-now).

**Approve semantics (fully specified — this is where real money moves):** plan TTL (default = min(horizon, end of session)); at tap, the chokepoint re-validates fresh — live quote inside the entry zone and above stop/invalidation, RiskEngine fresh pass, kill switch; outside the zone → the app renders "plan stale — refreshed check failed" and refuses; **never chase**. Fill = limit at zone top for longs, TIF day. Size = the stamped deterministic amount, which the fresh RiskEngine pass may shrink or refuse, and the card explains when it does.

**Daily wrap (ALWAYS sends on trading days, ~16:15 ET; it is the user-side heartbeat and the user is told to treat its absence as the down-signal):** ideas + resolutions + the lane meter ("214 events → 38 episodes → 31 investigations ($1.87) → 9 ideas (2 pushed · 4 digest · 3 silent) · attempted 31 / parsed 30 / failed 1 · shed 12 storm / 0 budget · all lanes alive"), suppressed-cohort outcomes ("suppressed cohort went 2/5 — the bar earned its keep"), promotion/demotion announcements. Every number is a ledger query. It **buzzes** only on ≥1 idea or a true anomaly (z-score vs trailing baseline — routine storm-shed evictions are expected-and-boring and must not buzz, or habituation kills the meter by week 3); otherwise inbox-only, but always present.

**Worked example.** 10:41 ET: the collector logs a $2.1M ask-side TSLA call sweep; a dark-pool block landed 10:31; RVOL 3.4×. Convergence fires; intake picks it off the store at 10:42 (cursor poll), scores 8x → episode opens, investigation fires immediately. Dossier: 9 claims, one contradiction (put skew rising). T1 null hypothesis: "possible hedge against the block — check expiry ladder." v1 registers *long TSLA 244.50–246.00, stop 239.80, T1 252, horizon 5d*, bear case addressing the null; the episode hash-selects for A/B, v2 registers its variant (`sample_reason=hash_ab`). Neither source is push-armed → one inbox card (higher tier primary, "second opinion differs: v1 stop 239.80 vs v2 240.60") attached to the day's existing TSLA convergence `group_key` — the phone does **not** buzz again; the 13:00 digest carries it labeled "unproven — reason_v1 apprentice (n=7)". Weeks later, at a fixed checkpoint, `reason_v1 × flow.convergence` holds cluster-adjusted Wilson-LB 58% with positive alpha LB and 74% fill rate across 5 distinct weeks; the founder gets a nomination card and arms it; the *next* such trigger arrives as an Approve/Reject card reading `win floor 58% · n=23 · fills 74%` — a number that was earned.

## Ledger + calibration integration (registration, grading, how reliability gates delivery)

**Registration at birth, before the delivery decision, everything:** REASON v1/v2/escalated ideas, structure_event fallbacks, T1-dismissal template shadows, drop-floor ε-shadows, nightly setup_scan (already wired), convergence mechanical ideas, and Path A proposals with `user_action` stamping. Suppression can never touch the denominator because registration precedes routing. No conviction filter at record time (spec §10). Registration is never throttled in storms — it is a cheap deterministic write, and storms are when the denominator matters most.

**The Grading Law (pre-registered; the single most load-bearing definition in the product):**
- **Estimand**: win = plan's T1 touched before stop within horizon, graded against the idea's own plan. **Same-bar stop+target → stop wins** (pessimistic tie-break, stated once, tested).
- **Fill conditioning**: entry zone never touched within the entry window → `no_fill` — **excluded from the win% denominator, never silently dropped**: fill rate is a first-class number rendered beside win% on every surface and gated alongside it, so a source cannot shrink its own denominator by posting chase-y zones without the shrinkage being visible. Each LLM idea additionally grades a shadow **market-at-trigger canonical-template variant**, so zone-placement and geometry drift (tighter T1s inflating win-to-T1 — the LLM authoring its own goalposts through the grader) is detected as divergence between realized-plan and canonical-template lines. Per-cell plan-geometry stats (median stop%, T1%) are monitored for drift.
- **Resolution**: event-driven intraday origins grade on **minute bars** (`research/intraday_bars.py` cache; throttled fetches over the small open-idea symbol set — never burst-request Schwab), stamped `grading_resolution=minute`; daily-horizon origins keep the existing daily-bar sweep with its next-day `_first_evaluable_date` (correct for the nightly scan; provably wrong for a 10:41 intraday long that hits T1 same-day and gaps through the stop overnight). Missing minute data falls back to daily **with the resolution flag stamped**; cells report their resolution mix; Schwab stays a soft dependency.
- **Benchmark**: `ret_pct / spy_ret_pct / alpha_pct / exit_reason` per spec §3, one adjusted series per resolution, NULL never zero-filled, `data_gap` recoverable.

**Calibration (spec §6 math + three statistical repairs):**
- **Cluster-effective n**: every idea carries `cluster_key = (direction, session-or-week bucket, macro-day tag)`; ten same-direction ideas minted in one squeeze are ~1–3 effective samples, and the Wilson interval runs on cluster-adjusted n_eff (Kish under decay handles time-weighting only, not cross-sectional correlation — both apply). MIN_N=10 applies to the raw count, the gate to n_eff. Samples must span ≥3 distinct weeks.
- **Fixed checkpoints, not first-crossing**: cells are evaluated for nomination only at n ∈ {10, 20, 40} or on the monthly schedule, and the gate must hold across 2 consecutive sweeps — killing the optional-stopping inflation of ~50 racing cells where "nominate at the data-dependent moment it first looks good" guarantees false arms.
- **Expectancy gates arming, never win% alone**: the arming recommendation requires the lower bound of expectancy (mean R, conservative payoffs) or alpha-vs-SPY > 0 *and* the Wilson LB floor *and* fill rate ≥ floor — a 75%-LB cell at R:R 0.4 is honest arithmetic describing a negative-EV product and must not earn pings.
- **Hierarchical cells**: origin-level cell is the fallback; split to origin×family (and regime, classifier-versioned — a regime-definition change bumps the version and starts fresh cells) only when each child clears MIN_N with disjoint CIs (the spec's regime rule, generalized). A multi-family episode's idea belongs to its **primary trigger class by rule** (highest-scoring family at open) — cell assignment ambiguity is itself a gaming surface.
- **Replay seeding (advisory only)**: detector stores replayed over their own recorded history pre-grade template ideas mechanically (no LLM); replay cells are labeled `replay`, inform triage priors and arming *recommendations*, and never supply a displayed number.

**Reliability gates delivery — the apprenticeship ladder:** every source starts **generation-on / push-off** (the strategy-off-by-default rule generalized): silent+digest-labeled-unproven from birth → **push** only when the founder arms it off a nomination card carrying the documented default policy (cluster-adjusted Wilson LB ≥ floor, expectancy LB > 0, fill ≥ 60%, n_eff ≥ 15 across ≥3 weeks, held 2 checkpoints — rendered next to the arm button so arming never rots into vibes). Demotion is symmetric but human: a cell whose Wilson *upper* bound falls below its stated floor suppresses the number and raises a founder review card — with **latency attribution shown first** (losses concentrated in high-queue-latency ideas indict the pipeline, not the source) — never silent auto-kill. Promotions/demotions are journaled and announced in the wrap: "flow+level promoted after 23 graded ideas" is itself a trust-building message. `stale_suppressed` ideas still grade; a rising stale rate flags the pipeline.

## Failure modes + how the design fails safe and OBSERVABLY

| Failure | Fail-safe | Observable as |
|---|---|---|
| LLM garbage / parse storm (the actual incident) | retry-with-repair → `parse_failed`, no fabricated fallback, graded vs template | rate-vs-baseline alarms (>20%/session or 3-consecutive) → founder push; attempted vs parsed in the wrap — zero and dead are different numbers |
| 90%-dead lane (partial rot) | nothing to prevent — impossible to hide | all liveness rules are **rates vs 10-day calendar-aware medians** read from daily counters, never booleans-at-zero, never current row status |

## The one thing this design gets right that obvious designs miss

**The pipeline's output is the ledger, not the ping — and silence, at every layer, is a manufactured artifact with a receipt, provable from outside the process that produces it.** The obvious design wires detector → LLM → push and fails four ways at once: it melts at the open (LLM inline with events), it drowns or starves the phone (every investigation wants to become a notification), it lets the LLM author its own report card (plans grade against goalposts the model writes, so "honest" Wilson numbers drift with prompt style), and — the failure this repo actually lived — it dies silently, because a chain whose job is pushing things looks healthy pushing nothing. Here the four records are decoupled and individually counted (trigger → investigation → idea → delivery), a conservation law forces every input to a terminal disposition, suppressed and dismissed work is graded so the bar's discarded alpha is a report rather than a mystery, the estimand is pinned (canonical-template shadows catch geometry gaming; cluster-n and fixed checkpoints keep "lower bound" from becoming a lie precisely when markets trend), and liveness terminates outside the failure domain — a canary that must physically ring a test phone, and a dead-man cron that escalates on a second channel when the daily wrap's delivery receipt is absent. A dead lane, a rotted parser, a wedged push token, and a genuinely quiet market all leave distinguishable fingerprints in the same meter the founder reads at 4:15 — so trust accrues from evidence the system cannot help but generate, which is the entire product.
