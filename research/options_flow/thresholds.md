## Honesty caveats

- **Regime coverage:** history is only ~2.5 years (≈ one bull regime). These percentiles tune UV-2 scale; they are not multi-regime validated parameters.
- **Survivorship tilt:** only *currently-listed* contracts are enumerable from Alpaca's contracts endpoint. Expired contracts that traded in the window are missing — a mild tilt on the baseline, acceptable because baselines only set the z-score scale.
- **No historical OI:** Alpaca exposes current open interest only. Backfilled `options_flow_daily.close_oi` rows are left **NULL**; vol/OI (UV-1) cannot be reconstructed from this backfill.

## Recommended `ZSCORE_STD_FLOOR`

**`ZSCORE_STD_FLOOR = 1242.65`**

Derived as `0.1` × the median of per-(underlying, DTE-bucket) daily-volume standard deviations across the backfill. UV-2 uses `z = (vol − mean) / max(std, floor)`. Without a floor, thin names whose trailing 20-day std collapses near zero produce huge z-scores on tiny absolute volume moves (false fires). A small fraction of the typical bucket std stops that without muting liquid names.
