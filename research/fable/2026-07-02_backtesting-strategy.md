## 1. What's been built — the research machinery
A competent, textbook-correct López de Prado pipeline, each piece small enough to audit:
- **Data:** `norgate_loader.py` (survivorship-free S&P universe, 910 syms 2010–2026, delisted stubs `SYM-YYYYMM`), `databento_loader.py` (10y true 1-min /ES, tick-verified), `bars.py`/`intraday_bars.py`, `regime.py` (FRED macro + point-in-time breadth, trailing SMAs, as-of lookups).
- **Labeling/sim:** `labeling.py` (triple-barrier, same-bar stop∧target = loss, conservative), `strategy_sim.py` (scale-out exits, `same_day_ambiguous` tracked, explicit note that −100% floor optimistically biases shorts).
- **Validation:** `validation.py` (purged K-fold + embargo, overlap on real label windows), `calibration.py` (OOF logistic → Platt), `ev.py` (Wilson CIs, `insufficient_data`, winsorized), `significance.py` (block bootstrap on entry-date-aggregated daily means, prints naive IID CI labeled "OVERSTATED"), `overfit.py` (Deflated Sharpe + PBO/CSCV).
- **Ship discipline:** `momentum_reference.py` + `fixtures/momentum_targets.json` (4 golden month-end target sets, 6-sig-fig, enforced by `tools/momentum_validate.py`); `fixtures/TACTICAL_FIXTURES.md` (14 golden VIX episodes, 5,705 steep-YC days). Bit-exact PC→serv port validation.
- **Tests:** synthetic, deterministic, no-network unit tests for essentially every module (24 on `profile_mr_swing` alone).

Genuinely rigorous, named: entry-date aggregation before bootstrapping (kills pseudo-replication); the excess-over-SPY re-test in round 2 of `EDGE_HUNT_FINDINGS.md` that demoted its own round-1 winners; Jensen-alpha decomposition that downgraded its own "strongest edge" (steep-YC → half beta-recovery, standalone α p=0.078 NS); PBO/DSR on the final book; golden-fixture handoff.

## 2. Per-strategy credibility
- **Residual momentum (70%) — credible sleeve, marginal stock-picker.** +0.47%/mo excess (CI [+0.14,+0.83]), 16/16 yrs gross-positive, survives ex-2020+2026 (p=0.022). Attribution: ~+0.18 EW/size tilt, +0.30 selection α that loses significance ex-crisis (p=0.118). Weaknesses: sample starts **2010** (no 2008 bear, no 2009 momentum crash — the canonical momentum killer is OUT of sample); Russell/mid-cap generalization untested. **Credible, with a known untested crash regime.**
- **Steep-YC dip (20%) — credible tactical beta, not alpha; team already knows.** Raw +0.98%/trade, n=13,102; beta 1.30, beta-adj α +0.47%/trade p=0.078 (ex-crisis p=0.27); top-beta tercile −0.03%. Ships the honest note ("NOT standalone alpha... tactical exposure"). - **VIX-spike (10%) — economically sensible, statistically unfalsifiable.** +3.34%/episode, 10/10 beat SPY — but **n=10 in 16 yrs**, knife-edge (>28 passes, >26 fails). Labeled "pilot," 10%. **Not-yet-shown, never provable live at 0.6 episodes/yr; acceptable at pilot size w/ economic rationale, which is how it ships.**
- **Combined book (70/20/10) — strongest claim, best-defended.** Sharpe 1.42 vs SPY 1.03, MaxDD −16.5% vs −23.3%, Jensen α +6.4%/yr at **beta 0.76** (t=3.6; ex-crisis +5.0%, t=3.0), PBO=0.000 over 12,870 CSCV splits, DSR 0.9997 at N=76, bootstrap Sharpe CI [0.91,2.01] → ~93% P(beats SPY). Caveats in-doc: 0.87 SPY corr ("better equity book, not market-neutral"); flat/inverted YC → Sharpe 0.69, excess **−0.83%** — *roughly the current regime*. **Credible — the deployable result — but see §4 reproducibility + 2010-start.**
- **Earnings-drift — real but softer than its label.** 6,624 setups, EV +0.89%/trade, CI [+0.43,+1.28] per-date, 16/17 yrs; +0.147 corr as 4th sleeve. But the earnings DB **omits delisted names** (acknowledged "upper bound" in findings, NOT flagged in shipped `strategy_stats.json` where it's "validated"). Gap-PEAD variant failed OOS, shelved. **Marginal-to-credible; "validated" overstates by one notch (survivorship gap).**
- **Profile-MR swing — correctly parked, exemplary process.** Clean 807-line backtest (prior-ISO-week anchors, regime filter on `bars[:idx]`, alpha-vs-beta ablation `--control`, true-1-min POC cross-check). Rejected **four ways**: naive intraday MR −EV; +6.5pt avg is 2025-dominated (strip 2025 → loses other 16 yrs, −484pt in 2022); beats regime-only control 7/17 yrs; true 1-min anchors don't change it. **No edge, honestly established; consider the "faithful swing" hope spent unless a new conditioning variable appears.**
- **Intraday-reversion / absorption-MR — cost-killed, correctly.** Net −0.87pt/trade, CI all-negative across 27 param combos; a 1-min candle can't distinguish absorption from breakdown (a *data* problem → don't tune). **Dead as framed; order-flow fork is optional.**

- **Short mirror — cleanest kill.** Best config CI [−0.07,+0.37] NS gross, borrow flips negative, sim acknowledged optimistic for shorts → strengthens a safety invariant.

## 3. What's being done well
Above-retail-grade + honest: (1) survivorship-free data as foundation; (2) overlap-aware significance (per-date agg + block bootstrap) as the standard gate; (3) the excess-over-SPY revision demoting its own winners (best sign of honesty here); (4) beta/Jensen decomposition reclassifying "strongest edge" as tactical beta *and shipping that framing*; (5) ablation controls; (6) cost discipline killing a founder-pet thesis ("absorption-MR — cost-killed") w/ explicit "chasing the least-bad corner = overfitting"; (7) PBO/DSR *after* the hunt on the selected book; (8) calibrated-never-fabricated in shipped code (`vix_spike` ships `win_rate: null`); (9) golden fixtures making PC→serv falsifiable; (10) negative results *committed* as documents. The declaration "backtesting is exhausted; the honest next step is forward paper, not more analysis" is the correct stopping rule most retail quants never reach.

## 4. What's wrong / the risks
1. **Headline results are NOT reproducible from the repo.** Sharpe 1.42, Jensen α, PBO=0.000, DSR — all produced by scratch code in `data/research/_edgehunt/` (**gitignored**). Committed evidence is a findings *document*. The book NAV backtest, CSCV/PBO, and CAPM regressions have **no committed, tested implementation** — a sign error in `capm_alpha.py` would never be caught by CI. Biggest gap between "we did rigorous work" and "the rigor is verifiable."
2. **Sample starts 2010.** One secular bull with V-shaped crashes. No 2008, no 2000–02 grind, no 2009 momentum crash — and the book is 70% momentum whose crash-protection overlay "killed the alpha" (so it ships *unprotected*). −16.5% MaxDD is conditioned on a friendly regime class.
3. **OOS is one split + exclusion cuts, not walk-forward.** Train ≤2019/test ≥2020 — and the OOS period is exactly the two crisis-rebounds that flatter dip/VIX. Doc admits "OOS strength is inflated by COVID + tariff-recovery" yet headline numbers stay full-sample.
4. **Threshold knife-edges.** YC>0.5 (fades >0.6, sits at ~0.5 now), VIX>28 (fails >26). Point thresholds selected on the same data that validated them; nothing averages across the plateau.
5. **Trial-count self-accounting.** DSR N=76 counts this hunt's hypotheses — not pre-hunt variant sweeps, hold-period grids, sleeve-weight choice (was 70/20/10 pre-specified?). Margin exists but the family is larger than the ledger.
6. **Label drift in shipped stats.** `dip_buy` "validated" at +0.59%/trade — true vs zero, but round 2 showed the unconditional dip **ties SPY** (beta + small MR kicker); note doesn't say so. `earnings_drift` "validated" with no survivorship caveat. These are the numbers the 6am proposal UI shows.
7. **Regime confound for paper.** The book's weak regime (flat/inverted YC) ≈ current regime → paper will *honestly* undershoot the backtest average even if nothing's broken. Without a pre-registered regime-conditional benchmark, paper can't distinguish "broken" from "dormant."
8. **Minor mechanics:** limit-fill optimism (touch=fill, no queue/adverse-selection); earnings backtest uses historical next-earnings dates while live uses forward calendar (vintage mismatch untested); momentum rebalance fill assumptions live only in gitignored code.

## 5. Fastest credible path to trust
**Reframe:** "years on paper" is only true if paper's job is to re-prove the edge from scratch. It shouldn't be. Per-sleeve live significance is hopeless (residual momentum needs ~9–10 *years* for t≈2; dip fires ~20×/yr; VIX ~0.6×/yr). The backtest — bootstrap, PBO, DSR — *is* the edge evidence. Paper/live verifies three cheaper things:
- **Implementation fidelity (weeks–months):** run the backtest engine forward as a *shadow sim* on live dates; compare every paper fill to its simulated twin (tracking error, slippage). 3 momentum cycles ≈ 300+ fills → slippage bounded by month 3. `MOMENTUM_PORTFOLIO_SPEC.md` already says "compare 2–3 cycles"; formalize tolerance bands in advance.
- **Sequential break-detection, not fixed-horizon proof:** pre-register the bootstrap return cone; monitor live NAV CUSUM/SPRT-style with a kill line (below ~5th pct → stop) and a consistency line. Detecting *broken* is fast; confirming positive α stays slow — fine, size is capped while trust accrues.
- **Regime-conditional benchmarking:** judge paper months vs the backtest's *same-regime* distribution (steep vs flat YC), not full-sample Sharpe 1.42.

Then: **validate at book level, not per-sleeve** (attribution is for ops/caps); use **earnings-drift as the statistical canary** (~400 setups/yr — the only strat that can approach live significance in ~a year, and it exercises the highest-vintage-risk pipeline); **graduate to small real money earlier than instinct says** (for liquid names paper fills add little over the shadow sim; the marginal lesson of tiny live size is behavioral/fill-quality, not statistical — via the Alpaca live double-gate, never Schwab). The decision-journal J3 loop is the right substrate.

**Cut:** further edge-hunting on the same 2010–2026 daily S&P data (hypothesis space converged); profile-MR resurrection (rejected 4 ways); absorption-MR without order-flow data; treating session-node as anything but an app-exerciser.

**Bottom line:** the earlier audit was wrong about the substance. This branch contains a real, honestly-tortured result — a low-beta equity book with ~+5–6%/yr Jensen alpha that survived excess-benchmark, beta-adjustment, crisis-exclusion, and PBO/DSR gauntlets, surrounded by an unusually candid trail of its own killed ideas. The credible worries are **reproducibility** (proof lives in gitignored scratch), a **bull-era-only sample** under a 70% momentum book, and **threshold fragility** — not fabrication or naive overfitting. Trust in *months* is achievable by validating fidelity and non-breakage against the pre-registered backtest, instead of asking paper to re-derive significance the market only hands out by the decade.
